Meritt

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

In short: your money data stays on your iPhone. Meritt has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing that ever leaves your device is feedback you choose to send.

What Meritt stores, and where

Everything you enter (payments, income, accounts, categories, notes, recurring entries and your settings) is stored in a database on your iPhone. Meritt's widgets, Siri and Shortcuts read that same on-device database. None of it is uploaded to a server, and the developer cannot see it.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Exports and backups

When you export a CSV file or a backup from Settings, Meritt creates the file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, iCloud Drive, a message) is your choice, and that service's own privacy terms apply.

App Lock

App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through iOS. Meritt only learns whether unlocking succeeded. It never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Notifications

Reminders for recurring entries are scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved.

Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Send Feedback and tap Send, Meritt sends:

  • the message you wrote,
  • your email address, only if you typed one in the optional "Reply to" field,
  • the app version and the iPhone model and iOS version (shown on screen before you send).

This is submitted to a Google Form and stored in the developer's Google account, so Google processes it under Google's Privacy Policy. It is used only to read and answer your feedback and to fix problems. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Ratings

"Rate Meritt" uses Apple's built-in rating prompt or opens the App Store. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

What Meritt does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Deleting your feedback

To have feedback you sent deleted, send another message through Send Feedback asking for it, with the email address you used (or enough of the original text to find it). It will be deleted within 30 days. Feedback without a deletion request is kept only as long as it's useful for improving the app.

Children

Meritt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of Meritt collects anything new, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use Settings → Send Feedback in the app.

Meritt is made by an independent developer.
